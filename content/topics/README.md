This folder is the canonical inventory of approved HOME article topics.

The inventory is split into numbered ranges so it can grow without mixing topic planning with the Spanish and English article files.

## Mandatory use

Before drafting, rewriting or validating a numbered article:

1. Read this file and the topic file containing the target `article_number`.
2. Use the exact approved topic/search intent for that number; do not invent, merge, replace, reorder or skip topics.
3. When checking whether a topic already exists, or when proposing future topics, search **all topic files in this folder**, not only the current range. This folder-wide check is required to reduce duplication and search-intent cannibalization.
4. Spanish and English versions share the same `article_number` and `translation_group`, while each language is written natively for its market.

## Animals, insects and household pest topics

HOME solves the household problem without framing animals or insects as things to kill or exterminate.

For these topics, preserve the underlying search intent but prefer prevention, exclusion, sanitation, removal of food/water/shelter, physical barriers, relocation or other non-lethal measures when they are practical and responsible. Titles and article prose should favor language such as `evitar que entren`, `hacer que dejen de aparecer`, `reducir la presencia`, `qué hacer si aparecen` and `cuándo llamar a un profesional` rather than `matar`, `exterminar` or equivalent wording.

Do not turn these articles into catalogues of poisons, traps or killing methods. When an infestation creates a material health, safety or structural risk — for example bed bugs, cockroaches, termites or a serious rodent problem — explain the risk clearly and recommend qualified professional control when prevention, exclusion or housekeeping measures are not enough.

This rule applies to Spanish and English localization: preserve SEO intent, but do not mechanically translate violent or extermination-oriented query wording into the published title or editorial voice.

The article body files remain separate from this inventory. When the article-generation/import pipeline is added, it should preserve this numbering and must not reorganize the topic inventory.
